Program Overview

Who This Program Is For

Our Anti-Bullying Program is designed for children and teenagers who are experiencing bullying at school or in their communities. Through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu principles, structured physical training and mindset coaching, participants develop confidence, emotional control and practical tools to handle conflict safely. This program focuses on prevention, awareness, self-respect and resilience — not violence — helping young people grow stronger mentally and physically.

What We Teach

Situational awareness, boundary setting, escape strategies, stress management, and practical self-defense techniques adapted to real-life scenarios.

Our Approach

Trauma-informed, supportive and non-intimidating training delivered in a safe, respectful environment focused on empowerment — not aggression.

Community & Support

We work alongside community organisations and support networks to create pathways to safety, confidence and long-term personal strength.
