Program Overview

Who This Program Is For

Our Anti-Bullying Program is designed for children and teenagers who are experiencing bullying, as well as those who may be engaging in bullying behaviors.

We work with students, families, schools, and educational institutions to address bullying at its roots — not only the consequences, but the causes behind it.

This program is suitable for individuals, school groups, and community-based initiatives seeking long-term, positive change.

What We Teach

Through age-appropriate Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu principles and structured physical training, participants learn:

  • Personal safety, self-awareness, and non-violent self-protection
  • Emotional regulation, impulse control, and conflict management
  • Respect, accountability, and healthy social interaction
  • How to respond to bullying situations safely and confidently — without escalation

The focus is not on fighting, but on understanding boundaries, responsibility, and self-control.

Our Approach

We believe bullying is a complex social issue that requires education, guidance, and support — not punishment alone.

Our approach is preventive, inclusive, and educational, helping children develop empathy, confidence, and emotional resilience.

For those who bully others, the program provides structure, discipline, and positive role models to redirect behavior and promote personal growth.

Community & Schools

We collaborate with schools, educators, parents, and local authorities to support anti-bullying strategies and create safer learning environments.

Programs can be adapted for schools, workshops, or ongoing community initiatives.
